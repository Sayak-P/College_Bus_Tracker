About CampusFlow

Built for ITER Campus

CampusFlow is a next-generation campus transit platform designed specifically for ITER (Institute of Technical Education and Research), Bhubaneswar. Our mission is to make campus commuting stress-free by connecting students and drivers through real-time technology.

Whether you're a student trying to catch your morning bus or a driver managing your route, CampusFlow provides the tools you need for a seamless transit experience.
